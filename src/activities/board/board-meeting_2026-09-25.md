* Council report (EBB): 
  * TEI's AI policy (accepting contributions made with AI) is being drafted
  * External funding possibilities: HumanityAI funding call is open. Eligible are US non-profit orgs, subject related to community AI models. EBB is happy to try it. Deadline is short but first round writeup requirements are limited. Decisions are expected Dec/Jan.
  * JC: another round just opened for the next year of Curiosity Awards (this is a rolling awards scheme). JC would need help in applying for this but it might be a good match.

Routing through an institution is often required and obviously an overhead would be consumed by the institution.
* Infrastructure / Website: 
  * A suggestion arrived via Infrastructure group's Slack from Peter Stadler who believes that the TEI-C should hire a webmaster, specifically for editing website content. 
  * MT: current website is really easy to change, so the issue is not a technical barrier. Currently we all feel reluctant to change virtually any content (except adding News items) without consulting others. Hiring someone will not address this issue. We also simply don't have the financial means.
  * JC: In order to avoid putting the extra burden on the Council, Board could take it as recurring task and devote some of the meetings to going through the website tickets and addressing content-related matters.
* TEI2027:

* TEI2028: 
  * JC has emailed the two candidates asking for a more detailed application. 
  * DG liaised with Prof. Christos Papatheodorou. The event would be hosted at the National Kapodistrian University, in the city center. Some costings can be obtained on the basis of previous events there. A question arrived what happens with the surplus money, does University keep it. JC: there was a change of policy, any surplus the organizers are free to keep, but there's no subvention from the TEI, unless the conference is unable to cover the cost.
  * JC: we should make a decision before Christmas, to give organizers time to start making arrangements. We want to come to 2027 with the announcement for 28, which implies representatives from 28 to be present in Tokyo in 27.
* OPM mid-term report (MT): 
  * OPM has been released on schedule, on Sep 15th. Sep 27th a steering committee meeting is planned to determine further focus, but so far we are even ahead of schedule.
  * Secondary objective for the e-editiones, but the most relevant for TEI-C, was to have the OPM able to process the TEI Guidelines to produce a static website with Guidelines prose and reference pages. This has been already achieved with the OPM, though some further review and improvements of behaviours required to produce elements of the reference pages will be carried out in the next weeks.
  * Findings so far were presented to the subgroup of the TEI Council on Sep 23rd, and an introductory session for interested Council members is being scheduled by MT.
  * From MT's perspective it would be helpful if Council agreed to prioritize the incoming PRs and PM-related issues. If TEI infrastructure is supposed to rely on the OPM, then the extensions required for the TEI infrastructure should be incorporated into the TEI vocabulary. OPM is scheduled to run for another half a year, so the Consortium should aim to keep this timeline to in mutual interest of both communities.
