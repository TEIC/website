# HumanityAI funding proposal 2026

> Work on this proposal has been spearheaded by Elisa Beshero Bondar, with generous support from Board and Council members over the course of October 2026. Results of the first round are expected early in the New Year.

## Proposal summary

Our organization’s resources are precarious and can support only one in-person meeting of the Technical Council for three days per year at our annual conference. This is sufficient to maintain the TEI in its current form, but insufficient to take the lead in designing new systems. We therefore seek support for additional meetings of the TEI Technical Council with community experts to work on new formulations of the TEI Guidelines and more resilient methods of delivery. 

Holding three additional meetings provides time to:
 * Evaluate security risks thoroughly and implement robust protocols to mitigate aggressive bot-scraping targeting our sites; 
 * Remodel the TEI Guidelines for improved machine processing, recognizing that the Guidelines’ language is already consumed by large language models and delivered in web search results through AI mediation;
 * Develop, test, and share AI processing tools with customizable open-weight language models to support cultural preservation work. 

Our main priority is to support in-person meetings in locations where TEI leaders are experimenting with emerging technologies. Several TEI members now research AI in separate projects within academic institutions, but if provided extended time and resources to work together, the TEI Consortium can pool our expertise to strengthen the TEI’s resources for the future. 

Requested funding would go primarily to:

 * support 3 TEI Council meetings over the next 3 years
 * support volunteers and invited experts in their work on grant deliverables and dissemination
 * support workshops for the community
