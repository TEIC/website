The Institute for Documentology and Scholarly Editing (IDE) is happy to<br/>
announce the launch of a new review journal, RIDE. We aim to make<br/>
digital editions and resources more visible and to provide a forum in<br/>
which expert peers can evaluate and discuss the efforts of digital<br/>
editors in order to improve current practices and advance future<br/>
developments. It will do so by asking its reviewers to pay attention not<br/>
only to the traditional virtues and vices of any edition, but also to<br/>
the evolving methodology and its technical implications.


<br/>
We would like to bring RIDE to the attention of TEI-L readers, as the<br/>
data is TEI encoded and available under CC-BY license.


You can access all reviews at: [http://ride.i-d-e.de](http://ride.i-d-e.de/)


You can find out more about the ideas behind RIDE, our methods and<br/>
intentions, in the Editorial at: <http://ride.i-d-e.de/about/editorial/>


We are also looking for contributions. Please see:<br/><http://ride.i-d-e.de/reviewers/>
