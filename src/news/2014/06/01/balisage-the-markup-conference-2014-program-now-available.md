Balisage is an annual peer-reiewed conference devoted to the theory and<br/>
practice of descriptive markup and related technologies for structuring<br/>
and managing information.


This year’s program includes: case studies from government, the standards<br/>
world, big data, and a web-browser game; change tracking and versioning in XML<br/>
documents; XSLT streamability; new approaches to overlapping data structures;<br/>
a discussion of MathML technology and practice; and technical papers covering<br/>
such topics as: Web Components, XForms for editing, RELAX NG for DITA, an<br/>
XQuery framework, OWL, identity in XML and JSON, and RDF and the Internet of Things.


Balisage participants typically include XML users; XSLT and XQuery programmers;<br/>
librarians; developers of XML databases, XSLT and XQuery engines, web publishing<br/>
applications, and markup-related software; semantic-Web evangelists,<br/>
archivists; members of the working groups which define markup specifications;<br/>
academics; industrial researchers, representatives of governmental bodies<br/>
and NGOs; consultants; and the world’s greatest concentration of markup theorists.


Discussion is open, candid, and unashamedly technical.<br/>
Balisage 2014 Program: <http://www.balisage.net/2014/Program.html>


Balisage will be located near Rockville, Maryland USA this year, accessible from the Washington DC Metro Red Line.
