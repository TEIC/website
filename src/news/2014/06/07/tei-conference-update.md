Dear Colleagues,


Here is an update on the planning for the 2014 TEI Conference, hosted by<br/>
Northwestern University in Evanston, Illinois, October 22-24,2014


The Program Committee has now completed its review of submissions. People<br/>
have been notified of the results of the review, and we expect to publish<br/>
a conference schedule within the next ten days. There are likely to be a<br/>
few changes at the margins, but what you will see on or about “Bloomsday”<br/>
(June 16) will be what you get.





Registration is now open at <https://www.conftool.net/tei2014/>.<br/>
Registration will be $150.00, or $120.00 if you register before August 31,<br/>
2014. Registration for graduate students will be $75.00, or $60.00 before<br/>August 31, 2014.


While hosted by Northwestern University, the physical venue of the<br/>
conference will be the Orrington Hotel, right on the edge of campus. Rooms<br/>
at the Orrington Hotel will be $179 a night. There are quite a few rooms<br/>
with two queen beds, and friends who like sharing a room may find this an<br/>
attractive option. More information about hotels will be forthcoming in<br/>
the weeks to come, and will be posted at the conference site<br/>[http://tei.northwestern.edu](http://tei.northwestern.edu/).


With best wishes for a good summer


Martin Mueller, Chair, Program Committee, 2014 TEI Conference<br/>
Professor emeritus of English and Classics<br/>
Northwestern University
