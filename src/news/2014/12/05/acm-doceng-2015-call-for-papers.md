CALL FOR PAPERS


Join us in Lausanne for the<br/>
15th ACM Symposium on Document Engineering (DocEng 2015),


September 8–11, 2015,<br/>

[http://www.doceng2015.org](http://www.doceng2015.org/)


———————————————————————–


<br/>
The 15th ACM Symposium on Document Engineering (DocEng 2015) seeks original research papers that focus on the design, implementation, development, management, use and evaluation of advanced systems where documents and document collections play a key role.


DocEng is a leading international forum for researchers, practitioners, developers and users to explore cutting-edge ideas and to exchange techniques, tools and experiences.


DocEng emphasizes innovative approaches to document engineering technology, use of documents and document collections in real world applications, novel principles, tools and processes that improve our ability to create, manage, maintain, share, and productively use these.


You are invited to submit original papers to the 15th ACM Symposium on Document Engineering (DocEng 2015), to be held at the Ecole Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne, Switzerland. Attendees at this international forum have interests that span all aspects of document engineering and applications.


DocEng is sponsored by ACM by means of the ACM SIGWEB Special Interest Group. Proceedings are available through the ACM Digital Library.


Symposium Chair: Christine Vanoirbeek (EPFL, Switzerland)<br/>
Program Chair:   Pierre Genevès (CNRS, France)


Submissions<br/>
===========


Authors are invited to submit original work in one of the following forms:


* Full papers describing complete works of research (up to 10 pages)<br/>
* Short papers describing smaller complete works of research or novel<br/>
challenges or visions (up to 4 pages)<br/>
* Application notes describing systems or tools of interest (up to 4 pages)<br/>
* Workshop/tutorial proposals: tutorials are by a domain expert and<br/>
introduce a current topic of interest to symposium attendees while workshops provide an informal setting in which to develop vision and exchange ideas in a specialized field of interest.


Accepted papers will be included in the DocEng proceedings and published in the ACM Digital Library.


Students presenting their work at DocEng 2015 will be eligible to apply for SIGWEB Student Travel Awards.


Important dates<br/>
===============


* Mar  9, 2015: Full paper abstracts due<br/>
* Mar 16, 2015: Full paper manuscripts due<br/>
* Mar 16, 2015: Workshop and tutorial proposals due<br/>
* May 21, 2015: Short papers and application notes due


Topics relevant to the symposium include (but are not limited to) =================================================================


Modeling and Representation:


* Document models and structures (multimedia, graphs, trees, streams,<br/>
Adaptive and responsive documents, probabilistic documents)<br/>
* Document representation and standards (interchange standards, markup<br/>
languages, style sheets, document type representation, metadata)<br/>
* Distributed documents<br/>
* Collaborative documents and sharing economy<br/>
* Document internationalization, multilingual representations


Generation, Manipulation and Presentation:


* Document authoring tools and systems<br/>
* Document presentation (typography, formatting, layout) algorithms and<br/>
systems<br/>
* Automatically generated documents, automated layout and composition,<br/>
variable data printing<br/>
* Documents for mobile devices<br/>
* Document transformation


Web Document Processing and Interaction:


* Rich web applications<br/>
* Systems and algorithms for safe and efficient document processing<br/>
* Scalable distributed document processing<br/>
* Linked data and semantics enrichment, linking techniques and standards<br/>
* Automatic tagging and semantic linking


Collections, Systems and Management:


* Document repositories, storage, indexing, retrieval, deduplication,<br/>
cleansing<br/>
* Massive collections of documents, large-scale processing<br/>
* Enterprise content management: models and standards, scale and performance<br/>
* Digital libraries and archives, preservation systems<br/>
* Document system components, versioning, synchronization<br/>
* Document systems and workflows


Security:


* Documents and privacy<br/>
* Secure document workflows, including policy and access<br/>
* Security printing, including document identification, tagging and meta-data<br/>
* Cyber-physical document workflows, especially electronic/print options<br/>
* Document access, workflow and security for mobile and printing devices


User Experience:


* Navigation, search<br/>
* Usability, accessibility, readability and aesthetics<br/>
* Collaborative authoring and editing, curation and annotation<br/>
* Workflows, integration and interaction between human and automated<br/>
processes<br/>
* Culture-dependent layouts


Applications:


We encourage submissions about document-centric applications such as:<br/>
* Web applications and systems<br/>
* Mobile applications<br/>
* Social networks<br/>
* Education<br/>
* Digital humanities<br/>
* Document analysis<br/>
* eBooks and digital publishing
