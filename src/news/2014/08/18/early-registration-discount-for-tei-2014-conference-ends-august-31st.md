Dear Colleague,


a quick reminder that the Early Registration discount of 20% for the 2014<br/>
TEI conference will end  August 31, 2014.If you register before<br/>
that deadline you save yourself a little money, and you give the<br/>
conference organizers very useful information about how many people to<br/>
plan for. For your sake and ours, please take advantage of the discount<br/>
and register at <https://www.conftool.net/tei2014/><br/><br/>
Conference papers will run from Wednesday morning (October 22) to Friday<br/>noon (October 24). The annual TEI members meeting is scheduled for Friday,<br/>
October 24, 9 am. A list of plenary sessions and  papers is available at<br/>
the conference web site <http://tei.northwestern.edu/keynote-speakers> and<br/><http://tei.northwestern.edu/papers/>. I expect to publish the actual<br/>
schedule in the second week of September.


In previous years, workshops have been held before the conference. This<br/>
year they will be held on Saturday, October 25, following the conference.<br/>
For a list of the workshops and their times, consult the program at<br/><http://tei.northwestern.edu/workshops/>


For hotel information consult <http://tei.northwestern.edu/local-info/>.<br/>
Getting from O’Hare airport to Evanston is uncomplicated and usually a<br/>
matter of less than an hour. More details about this later.


Best wishes for what little remains of the summer.


Martin Mueller, Chair, Program Committee, 2014 TEI Conference<br/>
Professor emeritus of English and Classics<br/>
Northwestern University
