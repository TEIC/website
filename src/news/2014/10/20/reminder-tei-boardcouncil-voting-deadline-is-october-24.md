Dear colleagues,


Reminder emails have just been sent via [opavote.org](http://opavote.org/) to registered electors in the TEI Board and Council elections who have not yet cast their votes. Voting will close at about 15:00 UTC on Friday, October 24 (the TEI Members meeting for business starts at 14:00 UTC, 9 a.m. Chicago time, and voting will be closed at the point in the agenda where results are to be announced).


Refer to [http://www.tei-c.org/Membership/Meetings/2014/mm70.xml](/membership/meetings/2014/mm70.xml) for candidate statements and information.


Please take the time to vote if you are eligible–the TEI Board and Council together determine the policies, focus, and technical details of the TEI Consortium and the Guidelines.


On behalf of David Sewell, election manager.


Yours in social media,<br/>
Paul O’Shea
