The Cologne Center for eHumanities (CCeH) at the University of Cologne (Germany) is offering a position for a


Digital Humanist / Software Developer<br/>
(3 years, TV-L 13, full-time with part-time option, starting 1 January 2015)


Tasks<br/>
Collaboration on different projects; among others an international EU-funded project aiming at providing access to archival material for academics and the public:<br/>
– implementation of open-source Web 2.0 features and tools<br/>
– usability of technical tools in the environment of digital archival material<br/>
– crowdsourcing tools for indexing and transcription on the basis of a web-based XML WYSIWYM<br/>
– gamification features<br/>
– development / enhancement / implementation of tools for image cropping, text image linking, image annotation (supported by tagging taxonomies, vocabularies and folksonomies)<br/>
– information enrichment (e.g. named entities) with authority files,


Required skills<br/>
– x-technologies (XML, XSLT, XQuery, XML-DB)<br/>
– web technologies (including deeper understanding of JavaScript)<br/>
– software development experience<br/>
– experience in distributed collaboration using git and issue tracking software<br/>
– dissemination & communication skills


Desirable skills<br/>
– additional programming languages (e.g. Java/C++, Ruby, Python, PHP)<br/>
– data and metadata standards in the Digital Humanities (CEI/TEI, EAD)<br/>
– digitization of cultural heritage objects (artifacts / images / texts)<br/>
– understanding humanities concepts and finding ways to realize them as technical solutions


A working knowledge / understanding of German should be acquired within 1 year.


Application Deadline: 17 November 2014


Applications should include a brief cover letter and a CV and may include links to projects. Applications must be sent electronically to [info-cceh@uni-koeln.de](mailto:info-cceh@uni-koeln.de).


For further information or in case of queries contact Dr. Patrick Sahle at [info-cceh@uni-koeln.de](mailto:info-cceh@uni-koeln.de).
