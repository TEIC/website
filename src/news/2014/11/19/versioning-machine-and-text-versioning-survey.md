Dear colleagues,


Karolina Badzmierowska, Roman Bleier and the VM team would be very grateful if you could complete a survey about The Versioning Machine and text<br/>
versioning.


The Versioning Machine (VM) is a framework and an interface for<br/>
displaying multiple versions of text encoded according to the Text<br/>
Encoding Initiative (TEI) Guidelines. Begun in 2000, there have been<br/>
several major updates since then.


Currently they are working on a new version (5.0)  and they are looking to<br/>
the community for feedback on the current version and feature requests<br/>
for the redevelopment.


We invite you to complete a short survey to guide the redevelopment.<br/>
There are a maximum of 27 questions, but it should not take longer than<br/>
ten minutes.


Here is the link to the survey:


<https://www.surveymonkey.com/s/VMSURVEY2014>


Many thanks on behalf of Karolina, Roman and the VM team.


Your social media coordinator,<br/>
Paul O’Shea
