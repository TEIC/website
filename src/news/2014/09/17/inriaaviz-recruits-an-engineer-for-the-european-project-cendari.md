INRIA/Aviz Recruits an Engineer for the European Project Cendari.<br/>
Requirements: Python/Django, ElasticSearch, HTML5/JavaScript/D3<br/>
The Aviz group at INRIA ([www.inria.fr](http://www.inria.fr/)) is seeking a talented and<br/>
motivated engineer to work on the European project Cendari<br/>
([www.cendari.eu](http://www.cendari.eu/)). Cendari is an infrastructure project aimed at<br/>
supporting historians and archivists working on the European history<br/>
with a transnational approach (by opposition of a national approach).


<br/>
The Aviz group, specialist in Visualization and Human-Computer<br/>
Interaction, is in charge of the Human-Computer Interfaces and<br/>
visualizations related to the Cendari project. To this aim, it seeks at<br/>
recruiting a motivated engineer proficient in the key technologies<br/>
chosen for the project: Python/Django, ElasticSearch, HTML5/CSS,<br/>
JavaScript and the D3 library, as well as semantic web technologies<br/>
(RDF, Triple-Store), web services, and standard back-end engines for<br/>
web-based systems.


The engineer will work in Orsay, France, inside a laboratory of INRIA,<br/>
the leading French Research Center in Computer Science, within the Aviz<br/>
group (see [www.aviz.fr](http://www.aviz.fr/) for a description of the research we conduct).


The engineer should be proficient in English, experienced in development<br/>
on the Linux operating system (Ubuntu), project management, Agile<br/>
software development, and development tools such as: git, wikis, issue<br/>
trackers, test suites, distribution and deployment of complex web-based<br/>
systems. Skills in visualization and HCI would be appreciated, as well<br/>
as understanding of XML, TEI, and EAD.<br/>
She/he will collaborate with other engineers at INRIA and partners of<br/>
the Cendari project throughout Europe (Ireland, Germany, UK, Italy, the<br/>
Netherlands, Czech Republic, Serbia, and France).


Contact: Jean-Daniel Fekete ([jean-daniel.fekete@inria.fr](mailto:jean-daniel.fekete@inria.fr)) with a<br/>
motivation letter, a CV, and recommendations.


Position starting date: November 1st, 2014


Position duration: 1 year


INRIA ([www.inria.fr](http://www.inria.fr/)) is among the most renowned research institution in<br/>
computer science worldwide. The Aviz group belongs to the INRIA Saclay<br/>
center (<http://www.inria.fr/centre/saclay>), located about 30km south of<br/>
Paris in the center of the “Plateau de Saclay”, an area dedicated to<br/>
research, especially in computer science, mathematics, physics, and<br/>
natural sciences. INRIA engineers earn 2100-3100 Euros monthly depending<br/>
on the level of skill and experience.
