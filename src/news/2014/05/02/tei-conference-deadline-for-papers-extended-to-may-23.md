We have extended the deadline for submissions to the 2014 TEI Conference until May 23, 2014. Send your submission to the Conftool site <https://www.conftool.net/tei2014/index.php?page=index>, where you also find instructions about the format.<br/><br/>
Since we live in a last-minute world, I don’t expect to receive many additional submissions until the deadline or possibly the day before. If you are thinking about submitting a paper or poster it would, however, be very helpful to the program committee if you signaled your intention right away. Send a brief email to teiconference2014@gmail.com with a working title or short paragraph about its topic.


The conference website is at http://tei.northwestern.edu.


With thanks in advance


Martin Mueller<br/>
Chair, 2014 TEI Conference Program Committee<br/>
Professor emeritus of English and Classics<br/>
Northwestern University
