We are inviting [applications to participate](http://members.tei-c.org/Events?eventId=880511&EventViewMode=EventDetails#cfp "Application process") in the [TEI Hackathon](https://wiki.tei-c.org/index.php/DH2014Hackathon "TEI Hackathon at DH2014 (wiki)") full day workshop that will be held on July 7, 2014, as a pre-conference session at [DH2014](http://dh2014.org/).







The goal of the hackathon is to make significant progress on a few projects during one day of work (from 9am to roughly 5.30pm).


For more information on the possible projects, the programme committee, and the application process, please follow this [link](http://members.tei-c.org/Events?eventId=880511&EventViewMode=EventDetails "link").
