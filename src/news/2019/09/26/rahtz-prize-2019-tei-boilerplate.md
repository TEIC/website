The TEI Consortium is pleased to announce this year’s winners of the Rahtz Prize for TEI Ingenuity. Congratulations to John Walsh, Grant Simpson, and Saeed Moaddeli for their excellent work on [TEI Boilerplate](http://dcl.ils.indiana.edu/teibp/index.html), a pathbreaker for the publication of XML/TEI on the web.
