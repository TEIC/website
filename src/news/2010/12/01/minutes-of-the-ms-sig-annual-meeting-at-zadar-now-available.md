Dear all,


It is already more than two weeks ago that the MS SIG had their annual<br/>
meeting during the TEI conference in Zadar. We had a lively discussion<br/>
with about 25 participants expressing their interest in manuscript<br/>
related encoding. The results in brief:


1. Review of the SIG’s work of the past year, in particular the new<br/>
module on genetic encoding<br/>
2. Work plan for 2011<br/>
a) Revision of Critical Apparatus<br/>
b) Extension of msDesc toward tboDesc<br/>
c) MSS ODD<br/>
d) Community Building / PR<br/>
3. Evaluation and discussion of the SIG’s survey on needs and<br/>
requirements of the community


You can find the minutes in our wiki:<br/>[http://wiki.tei-c.org/index.php/SIGMS_Minutes_20101112](https://wiki.tei-c.org/index.php/SIGMS_Minutes_20101112)


Please join the ongoing discussions!<br/>
All the best,<br/>
Malte
