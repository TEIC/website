The new version of oXygen has been released. It is available at the [oXygen website](http://www.oxygenxml.com).


The new version updates the TEI schemas and stylesheets to the latest versions. For the complete list of new additions and details please see [this page](http://www.oxygenxml.com/index.html#new-version).
