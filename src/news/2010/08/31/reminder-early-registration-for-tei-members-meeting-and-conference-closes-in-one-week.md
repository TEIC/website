A reminder that early registration for the TEI members meeting and<br/>
conference in Zadar, Croatia closes in one week. The conference will<br/>
consist of three days of optional but very exciting pre-conference<br/>
workshops (November 8-10), and three days of sessions, refereed papers,<br/>
and Special Interest Group meetings (November 11-14). Information on the<br/>
conference may be found here:<br/><http://ling.unizd.hr/~tei2010/index.en.html>. Registration is via our<br/>
online store: <http://tei-shop.org/>


If you register before September 8th, you will receive a 20% discount on<br/>
the pre-conference workshops and 15% discount on conference<br/>
registration. For members and individual subscribers to the TEI, this<br/>
discount is in addition to the up to 50% discounts on registration they<br/>
already receive.


Because workshops are being offered on a cost-recovery basis, it is<br/>
important that people who are considering taking a pre-conference<br/>
workshop indicate this soon: workshops that do not enroll sufficiently<br/>
are subject to cancellation by the end of September.


In addition to the exciting programme of speakers, our pre-conference<br/>
workshops are being conducted by prominent leaders in the world of TEI<br/>
and XML more generally: Norm Walsh (XProc), C. M. Sperberg-McQueen<br/>
(XQuery and Xforms), the TEI@Oxford team (Understanding ODD), Elena<br/>
Pierazzo and Malte Rehbein (the newly proposed Genetic Encoding Module),<br/>
and Andreas Witt, Thomas Schmidt, Hanna Hedeland, Timm Lehmberg (Using<br/>
TEI for Transcription of Speech).


Workshops are priced for members/subscribers at $60 for each half-day<br/>
($51/half-day before September 8th). Workshops vary in length from 1<br/>
half day to one and a half days.


You can become a subscriber of the TEI and/or register for the<br/>
conference workshops at our on-line store: <http://tei-shop.org/>. Early<br/>
Registration closes September 7th.
