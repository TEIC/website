[Issue 2](http://jtei.revues.org/367) of the [*Journal of the Text Encoding Initiative*](http://jtei.revues.org/), with selected papers from the 2010 TEI conference, has been published.


*jTEI*, the official journal of the Text Encoding Initiative, is an open-access peer-reviewed journal, with issues appearing at least annually.
