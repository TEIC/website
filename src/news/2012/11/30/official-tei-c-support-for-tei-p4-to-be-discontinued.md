Dear TEI Community,


When the TEI Consortium released TEI P5 version 1.0.0 in November<br/>
2007 it promised 5 more years of ongoing support for TEI P4.<br/>
We’ve reached the date where we are starting to remove this<br/>
support for TEI P4. By this we mean that we will no longer<br/>
respond to bug reports concerning it and will de-emphasise it on<br/>
the website by moving TEI P4 to the Vault alongside the earlier<br/>
versions. The locations for TEI P4 materials on the TEI-C website<br/>
will be permanently redirected to a location in the Vault.


This should have little or no effect on completed projects who<br/>
have large collections of TEI P4 documents. For ongoing projects,<br/>
if you regularly validate against a P4 DTD on the TEI-C website,<br/>
the old URLs for the DTDs will permanently redirect to the new<br/>
locations in the Vault.


The TEI-C Webmasters, David Sewell and Kevin Hawkins, will be<br/>
undertaking this work over the next few weeks. If you have any<br/>
questions about how this might affect you, please do ask!


As always, we encourage those starting projects to use the latest<br/>
version of the TEI Guidelines and those working with previous<br/>
versions of the TEI Guidelines to migrate content to TEI P5 where<br/>
feasible. For information on migrating from P4 to P5, see<br/>[http://www.tei-c.org/Guidelines/P5/migrate.xml](/guidelines/p5/migrate.xml).


Sincerely,
