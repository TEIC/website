The Text Encoding Initiative Consortium (TEI-C) invites nominations for election to the TEI-C Board, the Technical Council, and the TAPAS Advisory Board.


The following positions are vacant and up for election: 3 on the Board, 5  on the Council, a number yet to be determined on the TAPAS Advisory Board.


Please submit your nominations to the TEI-C Board Nominating Committee by 1st May 2018 through this [form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/1t-hN7_CwCA0a5dvHvrQsb6WqYQalhPnAr9eVONM8Klk/viewform).


More information can be found on the [Call for Nominations](https://wiki.tei-c.org/index.php/TEI-C_and_TAPAS_Call_for_Nominations_2018) page.
