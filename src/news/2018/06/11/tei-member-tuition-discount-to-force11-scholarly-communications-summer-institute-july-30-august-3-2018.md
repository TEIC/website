The Board is pleased to announce a new benefit for individual members of the TEI-C and for staff at institutional members of the TEI Consortium.


This year, the TEI-C is a partner organization of FSCI, and all TEI-C community members are eligible to receive a $150 tuition discount for <u>[FORCE11 Scholarly Communications Summer Institute](https://www.force11.org/fsci/2018)</u> (FSCI2018) hosted by the UC San Diego Library, July 30 – August 3, 2018 at the MET building on the campus of the University of California, San Diego, 9500 Gilman Drive, La Jolla, CA USA.


The code for this discount is TEI-MEMBER.
