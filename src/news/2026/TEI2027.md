title: "TEI2027 Conference"
date: 2026-09-28
---

[TEI2027](https://tei2027.tei-c.org), the annual conference and members’ meeting of the Text Encoding Initiative (TEI), will be hosted by Keio University in Tokyo, Japan from **30 August 2027 to 3 September 2027**. More details will be added to the conference website at [https://tei2027.tei-c.org/](https://tei2027.tei-c.org/) in due course.

Please note that Keio University has six campuses in Tokyo and Kanagawa Prefecture. The venue for the TEI Conference is the [Mita Campus in Minato-ku, Tokyo](https://www.keio.ac.jp/en/about/campus/mita/).

It is within walking distance of Tamachi Station (JR Yamanote Line / JR Keihin Tohoku Line), Mita Station (Toei Asakusa Line / Toei Mita Line), and Akabanebashi Station (Toei Oedo Line).

The conference will bring together researchers, developers, librarians, archivists, and other members of the international TEI community to share research, practices, tools, and ideas related to text encoding and digital scholarship.

Through presentations, workshops, posters, and community meetings, TEI2027 will provide an opportunity to discuss current developments in TEI and to strengthen connections across disciplines, languages, and regions.

Keio University looks forward to welcoming the TEI community to Tokyo in 2027.
