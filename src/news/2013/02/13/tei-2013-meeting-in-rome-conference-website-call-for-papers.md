Call for Papers, Posters and Panels


The Linked TEI: Text Encoding in the Web


2013 Annual Conference and Members’ Meeting of the TEI Consortium<br/>
2-5 October 2013<br/>
Università La Sapienza, Rome, Italy


* Deadline for submissions: March 30, 2013<br/>
* Workshop dates (tentative): 30 September – 2 October 2012 (see separate call)


The Programme Committee of the 2013 Annual Conference and Members<br/>
Meeting of the Text Encoding Initiative (TEI – http://www.tei-c.org)<br/>
Consortium invites individual paper proposals, panel sessions, poster<br/>
sessions, and tool demonstrations particularly, but not exclusively,<br/>
on the theme, broadly conceived, The Linked TEI: Text Encoding in the<br/>
Web.


Conference website: http://digilab2.let.uniroma1.it/teiconf2013/


**Submission Topics**


Topics might include but are not restricted to:


* TEI Conceptual Model
* TEI and semantic features
* TEI and formal ontologies
* TEI and semantic annotation
* TEI and the Semantic Web
* TEI schemas


Culture and scholarship


* TEI in different linguistic and cultural traditions
* TEI in libraries, archives and museums
* TEI and critical editing/text analysis/text corpora
* TEI and digital collections
* TEI and mass digitisation
* TEI and user studies


TEI Processing Model


* TEI tools (for analysis and publication) and infrastructures
* TEI and interchange
* Interoperability and integration with other technologies and standards
* TEI and visualisation


The future of the TEI


* Community and outreach
* Intellectual and technical challenges
* Social structure, sustainability and financial model


Submission Types


Individual paper presentations will be allocated 30 minutes: 20<br/>
minutes for delivery, and 10 minutes for questions & answers.


Panel sessions will be allocated 1.5 hours and may be of varied<br/>
formats, including:<br/>
three paper-panels: 3 papers on the same or related topics<br/>
round table discussion: 5-8 presenters on a single theme. Ample time<br/>
should be left for questions & answers after brief optional<br/>
presentations.


Posters (including tool demonstrations) will be presented during the<br/>
poster session. The local organizer will provide flip charts and<br/>
tables for poster session/tool demonstration presenters, along with<br/>
wireless internet access. Each poster presenter is expected to<br/>
participate in a slam immediately preceding the poster session.


Submission Procedure


All proposals should be submitted via an online platform, the<br/>
availability of which will be announced shortly. Please submit your<br/>
proposals by March 30, 2013.


If you don’t have already one, you will need to create an account<br/>
(i.e., username and password) in order to file a submission. For each<br/>
submission, you may upload files to the system after you have<br/>
completed filling out demographic data and the abstract.


Individual paper or poster proposals (including tool demonstrations):<br/>
Supporting materials (including graphics, multimedia, etc., or even a<br/>
copy of the complete paper) may be uploaded after the initial abstract<br/>
is submitted. Submission should be made in the form of an abstract of<br/>
750-1500 words (plus bibliography).


Panel sessions (three paper panels): The panel organizer submits a<br/>
proposal for the entire session, containing a 500-word introduction<br/>
explaining the overarching theme and rationale for the inclusion of<br/>
the papers, together with a 750-1500 words section for each panel<br/>
member.


Panel sessions (round table discussion): The panel organizer submits a<br/>
proposal of 750-1500 words describing the rationale for the discussion<br/>
and includes the list of panelists. Panelists need to be contacted by<br/>
the panel organizer and have expressed their willingness in<br/>
participation before submission.


All proposals will be reviewed by the program committee and selected<br/>
external reviewers.<br/>
Those interested in holding working paper sessions outside the meeting<br/>
session tracks should contact the meeting organizers at<br/>
meeting@tei-c.org to schedule a room.


Please send queries to meeting@tei-c.org


Conference submissions will be considered for conference proceedings,<br/>
edited as a special issue of the Journal of the Text Encoding<br/>
Initiative. Further details on the submission process will be<br/>
forthcoming.
