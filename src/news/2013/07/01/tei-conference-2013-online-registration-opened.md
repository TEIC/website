Dear TEI-L members,


we are pleased to announce that online registration for the TEI<br/>
Conference and Members meeting 2013 has now opened. This year’s TEI<br/>
Conference is hosted at the University of Roma La Sapienza, Italy,<br/>
with the support of AIUCD (Italian Association for Humanities<br/>
Computing and Digital Cultures) and will take place from from 2 to 5<br/>
October. As usual the Conference is preceded by three days of<br/>
workshops and tutorials from September 29 to October 2.


This year’s theme is: The Linked TEI: Text Encoding in the Web. The<br/>
programme includes keynote lectures by Allen Renear (professor and<br/>
interim Dean at GSLIS) and Marie-Luce Demonet (professor of French<br/>
Renaissance literature and director of the Maison des Sciences de<br/>
l’Homme Val de Loire), parallel sessions of papers, the annual TEI<br/>
business meeting, a poster session/tools demonstration and slam, and<br/>
special interest group (SIG) meetings. Details about the programme, as<br/>
well as infos about accommodation and local attractions are available<br/>
in the Conference website at<br/><http://digilab2.let.uniroma1.it/teiconf2013/>.


The rates and the online registration facilities are available at


<http://digilab2.let.uniroma1.it/teiconf2013/registration/>


We hope to meet you all in Rome!


Cordially,


Fabio Ciotti & Gianfranco Crupi


Local organizers TEI Conference 2013
