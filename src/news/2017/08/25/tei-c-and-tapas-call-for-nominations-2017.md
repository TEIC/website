The Text Encoding Initiative Consortium (TEI-C) invites nominations for election to the TEI-C Board, the Technical Council, and the TAPAS Advisory Board.


The following positions are vacant and up for election: 2 on the Board, 6 on the Council and 2 on the TAPAS Advisory Board.


Please submit your nominations to the TEI-C Board Nominating Committee by September 13, 2017 through this [form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/1KFWkH6Va3Kfc9opkuSQS_AZAEqgCxvEfNtreuCI9bC4/edit).


More information can be found on the [Call for Nominations](https://wiki.tei-c.org/index.php/TEI-C_and_TAPAS_Call_for_Nominations_2017) page.
