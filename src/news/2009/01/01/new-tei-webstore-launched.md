With great pleasure, the TEI announces the launch of its new [Webstore and Members Centre](http://www.tei-shop.org/ "Webstore and Members Centre") ([http://www.tei-shop.org/](http://www.tei-shop.org/ "http://www.tei-shop.org/")). For the<br/>
first time this will allow members to subscribe to the TEI as individuals and<br/>
register for the annual conference online, using a credit card.
