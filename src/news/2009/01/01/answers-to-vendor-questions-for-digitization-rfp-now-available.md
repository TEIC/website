The Text Encoding Initiative Consortium (TEI http://www.tei-c.org/) has 
released [answers](/Vault/Tite/RFPdocuments/RFP_Answers.html) to vendor 
questions submitted as part 
of [Requests for Proposals (RFP)](/Vault/Tite/RFPdocuments/RFP.html) 
competition for the provision of digitization services as a benefit to its 
institutional members.

The answers, with a link to survey data on our membership, can be found at 
<https://tei-c.org/Vault/Tite/RFPdocuments/RFP_Answers.html>.

For further information please contact [digitization@tei-c.org](mailto:digitization@tei-c.org)
