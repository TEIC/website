We are pleased to announce that we have opened registration for the Association for Documentary Editing’s 2016 Conference, “Celebrate the Vibrancy of Documentary Editing,” to be held in New Orleans, Louisiana, from August 4-6, 2016.  The draft schedule is available [here](http://www.documentaryediting.org/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2016/06/scheduleDRAFT.pdf).




To register, please visit [ this page](http://www.documentaryediting.org/wordpress/?page_id=1487).  The deadline for registration is 18 July.


See more in [the announcement on TEI-L](https://listserv.brown.edu/archives/cgi-bin/wa?A2=TEI-L;cdfc645d.1606).
