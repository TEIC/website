IAAS Annual Conference will be held at Trinity College Dublin on the 24th & 25th of April.


The Irish Association for American Studies is an all-island organisation that supports and promotes the study of the United States in Ireland. ‘Sight Unseen’ is a two-day interdisciplinary conference which will see academics from across Ireland, the UK, Europe, Canada, and the United States examine the theme of seeing, surveillance, and the visual sphere in American culture. [Dr. Lee Jenkins (UCC)](http://research.ucc.ie/profiles/A014/ljenkins/Publications) will give the Alan Graham Memorial Lecture on April 24th.


A full programme of events is available on the [IAAS website](http://iaas.ie/iaas-annual-conference/). Registration is now open and you can book your place [here](http://iaas.ie/annual-conference-registration/). The Peggy O’Brien Book Prize will be presented at the conference dinner on April 24th. If you wish to attend the dinner we would recommend booking your place in advance as spaces are limited. Any queries in relation to the conference can be directed to [iaasconference@gmail.com](mailto:iaasconference@gmail.com).
