**Scholarly Networks Colloquium**<br/>**April 16-18, 2015, Brown University**


The Virtual Humanities Lab in the Department of Italian Studies at<br/>
Brown University, in collaboration with the Center for Digital<br/>
Scholarship in the Brown University Library, and DARIAH-Italy (Digital<br/>
Research Infrastructure for the Arts and the Humanities), will host an<br/>
international colloquium entitled Scholarly Networks and the Emerging<br/>
Platforms for Humanities Research & Publication in the Patrick Ma<br/>
Digital Scholarship Lab at the John D. Rockefeller, Jr. Library from<br/>Thursday, April 16 through Saturday, April 18, 2015.





The three-day colloquium will explore the new types of scholarly<br/>
output produced when scholars use digital methods to collaborate on,<br/>
annotate and visualize traditional materials.


Stephen Downie, Professor and Associate Dean for Research, Library,<br/>
and Information Science at the University of Illinois and Co-Director<br/>
of the HathiTrust Research Center, will deliver the keynote address.<br/>
His talk, The HathiTrust Research Center: Bringing you 4.7 billion<br/>
pages of analytic opportunities! will take place on April 16 at 5:30<br/>
p.m, and is open to the public.


The colloquium proper is open to interested members of the public;<br/>
please register by emailing [italian_studies@brown.edu](mailto:italian_studies@brown.edu) by April 13.
