The organising committee for the annual conference of the Japanese<br/>
Association for Digital Humanities JADH2015 “Encoding Cultural Resources”,<br/>
to be held in Kyoto Sep. 1 to 3 later this year, is proud to announce the<br/>
launch of the conference website at [http://conf2015.jadh.org](http://conf2015.jadh.org/).


The Call For Papers is still open and scheduled to close one month from now<br/>
on May 7th, 2015.  Don’t miss this opportunity to join us for the latest on<br/>
Digital Humanities in Japans old capital Kyoto! Topics relevant to the TEI<br/>
are most welcome, even if there is no thematic connection to Japan.
