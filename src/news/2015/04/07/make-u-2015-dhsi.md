The MakerBus  [http://www.makerbus.ca](http://www.makerbus.ca/)  team is once again pairing up with<br/>
the folks at Eurekamp  <http://p4c.ualberta.ca/eurekamp/>  to bring a week<br/>
long summer camp for kids 8-12 to the University of Victoria in parallel<br/>
with DHSI. If you are traveling out to DHSI this summer during the week of<br/>June 8–12 and would like to bring your children with you, please consider<br/>
having them join us at MAKE U for a week of creative building, thinking,<br/>
and tinkering.


More information can be found here:<br/><http://dhmakerbus.com/2015/03/06/make-u-eurekamp-and-dhsi/>


If you have any questions regarding the camp, please email<br/>[info@dhmakerbus.com](mailto:info@dhmakerbus.com)
