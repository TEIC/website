The Austrian Centre for Digital Humanities/Institute for Corpus<br/>
Linguistics and Text Technology at the Austrian Academy of Sciences is<br/>
looking for a Data Curator (f/m) within the project “Joseph Eckhel (1737-1798) and his numismatic network”. This part-time position (50%, 20 hours/week) is available from<br/>March 15, 2015 for 6 months. Place of employment is Vienna.





Being conducted within the framework of Fontes Inediti Numismaticae<br/>
Antiquae (FINA), the project aims at transcribing and editing the<br/>
letters from and to Eckhel, which are mainly in French, Italian, Latin<br/>
and German, as well as providing a numismatic commentary on them. A<br/>
web-based digital edition will be published in cooperation with the<br/>
Austrian Centre for Digital Humanities.


Key duties<br/>
The successful candidate will be responsible for editing and enhancing<br/>
the XML data of the transcriptions according to the Guidelines for<br/>
Electronic Text Encoding and Interchange of the Text Encoding Initiative<br/>
in close cooperation with the project leader and the staff of the<br/>
Austrian Centre for Digital Humanities.


Skills required<br/>
– BA or MA in a historical or philological discipline<br/>
– Acquaintance with XML-based technologies, preferably practical<br/>
experience with TEI Guidelines (P5) and<br/>
– experience in philological text editing<br/>
– Knowledge of German, preferably also of Latin, Italian and/or French


The salary will be € 1.331,30 gross/month, according to the FWF scheme<br/>
(Austrian Science Fund). Applications (including CV) should be sent via<br/>
email to [acdh@oeaw.ac.at](mailto:acdh@oeaw.ac.at). The deadline is February 28, 2015.


For further information, please contact the project leader Bernhard<br/>
Woytek, or Daniel Schopper (ACDH).
