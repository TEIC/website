Digital Humanities at Oxford Summer School<br/>20 – 24 July 2015<br/>
Scholarship — Application — Community


Poster proposals for those planning on<br/>
attending DHOxSS 2015 (or members of the University of Oxford not<br/>
attending DHOxSS) should be submitted by Monday 18 May 2015. All<br/>
submissions (max 250 words) will be peer-reviewed by the DHOxSS<br/>
Organisation Committee and subject specialists. We will notify<br/>
applicants of the outcome before the end of May. The Poster session is at the welcome drinks reception the evening of Monday 20 July 2015.


<br/>
See<br/><http://dhoxss.humanities.ox.ac.uk/2015/ml/programme.html#posters><br/>
for more information.


====<br/>
Do you work in the Humanities or support people who do?


Are you interested in how the digital can help your research?


Come and learn from experts with participants from around the<br/>
world, from every field and career stage, to develop your<br/>
knowledge and acquire new skills


Immerse yourself for a week in one of our 8 workshop strands, and<br/>
widen your horizons through the keynote and additional sessions


Workshops:<br/>
– An Introduction to Digital Humanities<br/>
– Crowdsourcing for Academic, Library and Museum Environments<br/>
– Digital Approaches in Medieval and Renaissance Studies<br/>
– Digital Musicology<br/>
– From Text to Tech<br/>
– Humanities Data: Curation, Analysis, Access, and Reuse<br/>
– Leveraging the Text Encoding Initiative<br/>
– Linked Data for the Humanities


Keynote Speakers:<br/>
– Jane Winters, Institute of Historical Research, University of London<br/>
– James Loxley, University of Edinburgh


Additional Lectures:<br/>
Supplement your chosen workshop with a choice from 9 additional<br/>
morning sessions covering a variety of Digital Humanities topics.


Evening Events:<br/>
Join us for events every evening, include a research poster and<br/>
drinks reception, guided walking tour of Oxford, the annual TORCH<br/>
Digital Humanities lecture, and a dinner at Exeter College.


For more information see: <http://dhoxss.humanities.ox.ac.uk/2015/ml/>
