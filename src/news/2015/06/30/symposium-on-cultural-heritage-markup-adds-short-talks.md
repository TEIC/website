**SYMPOSIUM ON CULTURAL HERITAGE MARKUP ADDS SHORT TALKS**


With the addition of six short talks, the program for the symposium on Cultural Heritage Markup is now complete.


The short talks are:


To those who startle at innovation…<br/>
Laura Randall, NCBI/NLM/NIH


EAGLE and EUROPEANA: architecture problems for aggregation and harmonization<br/>
Pietro Maria Liuzzo, Ruprecht-Karls-Universität Heidelberg


<br/>
Encoding western and non-western names for Ancient Syriac authors<br/>
Nathan P. Gibson, Vanderbilt Divinity School<br/>
Winona Salesky, Independent Consultant for Syriaca.org


Duplicitous Diabolos: Parallel witness encoding in quantitative studies of Coptic manuscripts<br/>
Amir Zeldes, Georgetown University


Encoding document and text in the Shelley-Godwin Archive<br/>
Raffaele Viglianti, MITH – University of Maryland


Divide and conquer: can we handle complex markup simply?<br/>
Robin La Fontaine, DeltaXML

The symposium on Cultural Heritage Markup precedes Balisage: The Markup Conference 2015.<br/>
Balisage is the XML Geek-fest; the annual gathering of people who design markup and markup-based applications; who develop XML specifications, standards, and tools; the people who read and write, books about publishing technologies in general and XML in particular; and super-users of XML and related technologies. You can read about the Balisage 2015 conference at [http://www.balisage.net](http://www.balisage.net/).
