Please, find attached the complete program and registration information for our Digital Humanities Summer School at LINHD-UNED. This year it is devoted to Digital Scholarly Editing and it is sponsored by the Dixit Scholarly Editing Network. The course can be followed presentially or virtually (completely online!).<u></u><u></u>



Dates: 13-17<sup>th</sup> July 2015<u></u><u></u>



Place: Salón de Actos Facultad de Económicas, UNED, Madrid – or your own computer…<u></u><u></u>



More information registration process and program: <http://linhd.uned.es/en/p/summer_school_2015-2/><u></u><u></u>



Discounts apply for any student and member of DH Associations!
