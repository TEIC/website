Dear colleagues,


The following call for papers from *[Aigne](http://www.ucc.ie/en/aigne/)* may be of interest to some of you. Please follow the embedded [link ](http://www.ucc.ie/en/aigne/news/)for details of the CFP.


The deadline for submission is **Sunday 31st May 2015**.


As acting Editor-in-Chief of *Aigne*, I would greatly appreciate if you could distribute the CFP for *Aigne *amongst your colleagues and postgraduate students.


The CFP is available for download as [PDF](http://www.ucc.ie/en/media/electronicjournals/aigne/CFP_Imaginary.docx.pdf) on the *Aigne *website. Please feel free to download and print off the CFP and post it on notice boards in your department or postgraduate research facilities.


On behalf of myself and the *Aigne* team, thank you for your kind attention. We look forward to receiving your submissions.


Kind regards,<br/>
Paul O’Shea
