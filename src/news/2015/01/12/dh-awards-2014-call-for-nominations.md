<http://dhawards.org/dhawards2014/nominations/>


Please Forward! There are also translations of this announcement<br/>

===


<br/>
The annual open DH Awards 2014 is now accepting nominations!<br/>
Please nominate any Digital Humanities resource in any language<br/>
that you feel deserves to win in any of this year’s categories.<br/>
The open DH Awards 2014 are openly nominated by the community and<br/>
openly voted for by the public as a DH awareness activity.<br/>
Although the working language of DH Awards is English,<br/>
nominations may be for any resource in any language. Awards are<br/>
not specific to geography, language, conference, organization or<br/>
field of humanities. There are no financial prizes, just the<br/>
honour of having won and an icon for your website.


Nominations will be open until 2015-01-18. Voting will take place<br/>
shortly after.


Please note that the nominations must be for<br/>
projects/resources/sites that were<br/>
launched/finished/update/created in 2014.


To nominate something for the DH Awards 2014 visit the<br/>
nominations page at: <http://dhawards.org/dhawards2014/nominations/>


The categories for the open Digital Humanities Awards 2014 are:


– Best Use of DH for Fun<br/>
– Best DH Data Visualization<br/>
– Best Exploration of DH Failure<br/>
– Best DH Blog Post or Series of Posts<br/>
– Best Use DH Public Engagement<br/>
– Best DH tool or Suite of Tools


Again, to nominate something for the DH Awards 2014 visit the<br/>
nominations page at:<http://dhawards.org/dhawards2014/nominations/>


If you have any questions please see<br/><http://dhawards.org/dhawards2014/faqs2014/> or ask at<br/>[james@dhawards.org](mailto:james@dhawards.org) or tweet @DHawards
